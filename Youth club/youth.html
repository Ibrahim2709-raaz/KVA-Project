Tryout readiness

Know what to expect.

Tryout schedules and registration windows change by season. Contact KVA for the current dates, age groups, locations, and registration link.

  • Arrive early and ready to move
  • Bring court shoes, water, and athletic clothing
  • Expect skill work, small-sided play, and team evaluation
  • Coaches consider technique, decision-making, effort, and coachability
