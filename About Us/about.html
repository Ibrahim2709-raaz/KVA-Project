About KVA

Volleyball with purpose.

We create structured, welcoming opportunities for Kamloops athletes to develop skills, confidence, character, and a lasting connection to the sport.

Our story

Built for athletes. Strengthened by community.

KVA was created to meet the need for structured, accessible, high-quality volleyball in Kamloops. Our programs bring together athlete-centred coaching, clear development pathways, and a culture that values integrity and teamwork.

Across youth and adult programs, we aim to make every practice and match a place to improve, contribute, and belong.

Coaching matters

Positive leadership, clear standards.

Great coaching combines knowledge of the game with communication, patience, and care for the individual athlete.

  • Age-appropriate instruction and progression
  • Constructive feedback and accountable team culture
  • Support for confidence, resilience, and decision-making
  • A safe environment where athletes can challenge themselves
